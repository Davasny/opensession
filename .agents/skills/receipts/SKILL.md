Sum up the bug fix from this conversation in three short lines, plain language, no jargon:

- **Bug:** what was broken and why, in one sentence.
- **Fix:** what you changed, in one sentence.
- **Proof:** how you showed the fix would have prevented the bug, in one sentence. Say whether you reproduced the bug before the fix and saw it gone after, or only reasoned about it, or added a test (and whether that test fails without the fix). If you did not prove it, say so plainly.

Nothing else. No headings, no preamble, no caveats beyond the proof line.
